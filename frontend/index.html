1. What is CITYSYNC in Simple Words?

Think of CITYSYNC as the central nervous system or brain for an entire city. In most cities today, Traffic Police, the Electricity Board, and the Water Department operate in separate silos without talking to each other. When an electrical substation trips, water pumps shut down and traffic lights go dead, causing complete gridlock. CITYSYNC connects all these systems into one real-time 3D screen, predicting problems before they spread and telling city managers exactly what to do.

2. The Bengaluru Municipal Digital Twin

🇮🇳 Greater Bengaluru Operational Digital Twin Tactical Real-Time

A full tactical smart city platform monitoring 18 key physical infrastructure assets across Central Silk Board, Electronic City, Whitefield, Vidhana Soudha, and Peenya Industrial Hub, with 2D GIS map, 3D textured urban twin, and cross-system cascading impact chains.

4. Plain-English Metric Guide

AQI (Air Quality Index)
Score measuring air pollution. 0-50 is "Good" (healthy to breathe), 51-100 is "Moderate", 100+ is polluted.
Congestion Index (%)
How packed the roads are. Above 70% means heavy traffic jams where vehicle speeds drop below 20 km/h.
Digital Twin Platform
A real-time 3D computer model of the city that mirrors roads, buildings, power, and water systems with live sensor feeds.
Built with FastAPI, Three.js 3D WebGL, Leaflet GIS, and Machine Learning.
