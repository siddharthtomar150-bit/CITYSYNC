1. What is CITYSYNC in Simple Words?

Think of CITYSYNC as the central nervous system or brain for an entire city. In most cities today, Traffic Police, the Electricity Board, and the Water Department operate in separate silos without talking to each other. When an electrical substation trips, water pumps shut down and traffic lights go dead, causing complete gridlock. CITYSYNC connects all these systems into one real-time 3D screen, predicting problems before they spread and telling city managers exactly what to do.
